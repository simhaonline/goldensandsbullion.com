Leading the Way in Precious Metals Trading

Golden Sands Bullion Trading L.L.C-FZ is a premier precious metals trading company established in the heart of Dubai, one of the world's most important gold trading hubs. Our company operates under the prestigious Meydan Free Zone regulations, ensuring the highest standards of compliance and professionalism.

We specialize in the trading of non-manufactured precious metals, offering our clients access to the finest quality gold, silver, and other precious metals. Our commitment to excellence, transparency, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in the industry.

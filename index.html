Our trading desk streams real-time spot rates from global interbank liquidity providers for the XAU/USD symbol (1 fine troy ounce of gold). Because the United Arab Emirates Dirham is legally pegged to the US Dollar at 1 USD = 3.6725 AED, and 1 troy ounce equals exactly 31.1034768 grams, every per-gram price (24K, 22K, 21K, 18K) is computed with mathematical precision without hidden currency margins.

Furthermore, we are registered with the UAE Federal Tax Authority (TRN: 105588382900001) and Dubai Customs under Mirsal Business Code AE-1216587.

For private clients, our standard minimum order begins at 100 grams for fine gold bars or 1 kilogram for investment silver. For institutional and wholesale trading accounts, orders typically range from 1 kilogram cast bars up to 100+ kilogram palletized shipments with tiered wholesale spreads.

Through our officially registered Dubai Customs Mirsal Code (AE-1216587), our trade operations team prepares all export documentation, customs declarations, and airside logistics through Dubai International Airport (DXB). Armored transport is coordinated in partnership with Brink's, Malca-Amit, or Transguard with full insurance coverage.

In accordance with UAE Cabinet Decision No. 25 of 2018, pure investment precious metals (gold, silver, and platinum of 99% purity or greater) qualify for a 0% VAT rate (reverse charge mechanism between registered taxable entities). This provides significant fiscal efficiency for global institutional investors trading through Dubai.

We exclusively supply bullion refined by accredited LBMA (London Bullion Market Association) and Dubai Good Delivery (DGD) refiners. Each bar features an official refiner hallmark, stamped fineness (999.9), unique serial number, and an individual assay certificate. On-site verification via XRF spectrometry is available upon request at our trading facilities.
